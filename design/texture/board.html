Warm stock.

Wada No. 295 inks on uncoated cream paper, in late-afternoon window light. The photos are printed on the same paper, in the same light, so they belong to the page instead of sitting on it.

1. Paper

One grain on every section surface, alpha over the section’s own colour. Left half of each swatch is today’s flat colour, right half is with the grain. Fourth panel: the grain at 3x, to show its tooth, mottle and fibres.

2. Photo grade

One grade for every photo: black lifts to the palette’s ink, white settles on the page’s paper, the mids catch a little sun, colour eases back 12%. Then the page’s grain goes on top. Left: the Pexels original. Right: graded, with grain.

3. Light

One warm source, upper right. Where a photo meets the paper, a soft wash of Cream Yellow (--sun, #fdbf68) spills from it onto the page, so its light carries into the layout. Light only, never a flat fill; yellow stays with the sale.

Without the spill, the photo stops at its edge.

Rules

  • Grain on sections and photos only. The phone, storefronts, posts, forms and cards stay clean, like screens laid on the paper.
  • One scale: a 384px tile at 1x. It scrolls with the page and never moves on its own.
  • Photos bleed off at least one edge of the viewport or fill their column. Square corners, no frames, no shadows.
  • The 3D phone stands across a photo’s edge, half on the photo, half on the paper.
  • --sun is a radial wash at 12 to 22%, beside photos and in the hero. Never a flat block behind text.
  • Not this: torn edges, tape, stickers, stamps, halftone, film borders, vignettes, light leaks, moving grain.
