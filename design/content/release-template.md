# Model and photo release: example creator on comarket.si

A short release for a real person who agrees to play one of the fictional example creators on the Comarket site. Fill
the brackets, send it as a PDF or an e-signature request, and keep the signed copy with the photos (file it next to
`design/content/real-creators.json`, never in `public/`). One release per person. If the photographer is someone other
than the person, they sign the photo licence at the bottom too.

This is a plain-language template, not legal advice. Have it read once by a lawyer before the first one goes out.

---

**Release**

Between **[Your full name]** ("you") and **[Comarket legal entity name]**, [address] ("Comarket").

1. **What you're agreeing to.** Comarket may use the photos and short videos of you listed below (the "Images") on
   its website comarket.si, its subdomains, and in posts, ads and emails that promote Comarket, for as long as Comarket
   uses that example.

2. **How you'll appear.** You'll appear as a fictional example creator named **[example name, e.g. Alma B.]** with a
   made-up handle, audience size, post and quote, recommending a made-up brand (**[example brand]**). The page labels it
   "Example". The names, numbers, brands and words are invented by Comarket; they are not your real views, accounts or
   results. Comarket may crop, resize, colour-correct and place the Images inside drawn phone screens, posts and
   storefronts, and add text next to or over them.

3. **What Comarket won't do.** Comarket won't use the Images to suggest you personally use, endorse or are paid by any
   real company, won't attach your real name or real handles unless you say so in writing, won't use the Images in
   anything sexual, political, medical or defamatory, won't sell or license the Images to anyone else, and won't use
   them to train AI or to make AI-generated likenesses of you.

4. **Payment.** [Choose one: "No payment." / "A free Comarket storefront and a featured spot on the waitlist." /

5. **Taking it back.** You can ask Comarket to stop using the Images at any time by emailing [contact address].
   Comarket will take them off its site within 30 days. Copies already shared, cached or printed before then can't be
   recalled.

6. **Your age.** You confirm you are 18 or older and free to sign this.

## Asking a photographer for written permission (Unsplash, Pexels or Kaboompics)

The free licences on these sites forbid implying endorsement, so a photo from them can only play an example creator
with the photographer's written permission **and** the model's own signed release (the photographer's permission
alone doesn't cover the person's likeness). Send this; proceed only when both come back in writing.

> Subject: Permission to use your photo [link] on a labelled example
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I run Comarket (comarket.si), a site that matches brands with small creators. I'd like to use your photo [link]
> (and, if you have them, one or two more from the same shoot) to show a fictional example creator: a made-up name and
> handle recommending a made-up brand, inside a drawn phone screen, clearly labelled "Example" on the page.
>
> Your site's licence doesn't allow implying endorsement, so I'm asking first. Would you grant written permission for
> this use? And did the person in the photo sign a model release that allows commercial use like this? If so, could
> you forward this to them, or send me a copy of the release? I can send them a short release of our own instead
> (attached), and I'm happy to credit you on the page.
>
> We'd use it on the website and in posts that promote it, never to suggest the person uses a real product, and we'd
> take it down within 30 days if either of you asks.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name], Comarket
